## Corrections and limits

- Parent Help: official **0800 568 856**, replacing the PlunketLine number repeated in the PDF.
- Suicide Crisis Helpline: official **0508 828 865**, correcting the mixed prefix printed in the PDF.
- Rabbit Patch Greers Road: official **161 Greers Road, 03 358 2549**. The PDF phone is its Saffron Street centre.
- BestStart: individual official centre pages supply missing addresses, phone numbers and hours.
- Jellie Park: current Council notice reports indoor-pool maintenance closures until 2027; the old PDF opening hours are not used.
- Gordon Scouts: official group page fills section times and age bands. Trials do not make the club a free activity.
- Nor’West Brass: official website supplies rehearsal venue and role email; the correspondence address is not substituted for the rehearsal venue.
- Chinese Lifeline and CAF: no unverified dedicated phone numbers are promoted. Cards explicitly say availability/current contacts need confirmation.
- Facebook: Creative Capers public programme artwork is used with its page credit. Login screens, search results and guessed page names are not treated as verification.
- Broken/parked Spitfire pharmacy domain is replaced with the provider-reviewed Healthpoint page. Kendal Pharmacy also links to its provider-reviewed listing.
- Fees, opening hours and contacts not explicitly corrected remain dated July 2025 information. The source notice on every imported listing explains this. Named personal mobile contacts are omitted; public role inboxes and published service numbers are used.
- No capacity, accessibility or kitchen equipment is inferred for newly imported hire spaces. Unknown prices are not labelled Free. Conditional funded ECE hours, free trials and introductory coaching do not qualify the whole listing as Free.

## Graphics

44 new local assets were downloaded from public organisation websites or Creative Capers’ public Facebook page, visually reviewed and credited. Existing parent-organisation graphics are reused where appropriate. 69 imported records have credited graphics; 34 use explicitly identified BBFind illustrations because a suitable verified public graphic was unavailable. Parent-brand graphics do not imply that an organisation supplied, approved or endorsed the listing. No generic photograph is presented as a photograph of a local programme.

Raster downloads are resized to at most 800 × 500, encoded as WebP and stripped of source metadata. Static SVG marks retain their original artwork. Assets are served locally, not hotlinked to social networks or tracking URLs.
