# BBCN branding reference

Verified from https://bbcn.org.nz/ on 27 October 2026.

The public website's organisation configuration identifies **Burnside and Bryndwr Community Network**, with brand green **#138a59**. Its official organisation mark was downloaded unmodified from the public `avatar_url` on BBCN's Hail-hosted website and is included at `public/branding/bbcn-logo.png`.

BBFind retains a distinctive directory identity and original community illustrations. It uses BBCN green for navigation accents and identifies the parent organisation with its actual mark in the footer. Headline green is darkened to maintain accessible text contrast. Self-hosted DM Sans and Manrope provide directory typography; they are not presented as BBCN's official fonts.

The BBCN mark remains BBCN branding; inclusion does not transfer ownership or grant reuse rights to other projects. Fictional demonstration listings and original illustrations are labelled and are not copied from BBCN organisations' photographs. User-uploaded listing photographs go through a separate private approval process.
